Game Info

Welcome to the Bomber Game!

How to Play

  • Movement: Use WASD or the Arrow Keys to move your character around the map.
  • Bombs: Place bombs to destroy brick tiles and clear paths.
  • Power-Ups: Eating a power-up increases your bomb’s explosion radius by +1.
  • Objective: Search for the hidden Key inside one of the brick tiles. Once you collect the Key, a Port will spawn on the map. Carry the Key to the Port to complete the level.

Watch Out!

Enemies may be hiding behind the bricks. Destroying a tile could reveal a dangerous foe, so be careful where you bomb! Touching an enemy or being caught in an explosion will cause you to lose.

Tips

  • Plan your bombs carefully—explosions spread in a cross shape.
  • Use power-ups wisely to cover more ground, but remember, larger blasts can also put you at risk.
  • Always keep an escape route in mind when placing bombs.

Good luck, and happy bombing!
